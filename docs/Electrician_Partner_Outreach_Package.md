# SmartByte Group, LLC — High-Voltage Electrician Partner Package

## Overview
High-voltage electrical contractors frequently get asked by residential and commercial clients to install low-voltage data cabling, security cameras, Wi-Fi access points, or audio/video systems. Many electricians prefer not to handle low-voltage due to different tools, testing equipment, and margins.

SmartByte Group, LLC operates as your **trusted low-voltage partner** across the Kansas City metro area.

## The Partnership Model

### Option A: Direct Client Referral (10% Referral Fee)
- You pass us the lead for low-voltage / AV / networking / security.
- We quote and execute the job directly with the client.
- **We pay you a 10% referral fee** on labor upon job completion.

### Option B: Sub-Contractor White-Label
- You include our low-voltage scope directly inside your master bid to the client.
- SmartByte performs the installation as your specialized low-voltage sub-contractor.
- You mark up our sub-contractor quote to the client.
