# Personal LinkedIn Growth Strategy — Vince, Founder of SmartByteKC

## Goal
Establish Vince as a Kansas City thought leader in the smart-home and low-voltage networking space, positioning SmartByteKC as the premier "trusted neighborhood expert."

## Growth Tactics (Rapid Network Expansion)
1. **The "KC Connection" Strategy**: Engage daily with 5-10 local business leaders in the construction, real estate, and tech sectors in KC. Comment *thoughtfully* (no "great post") on their content.
2. **Influencer Mapping**: Identify 5 key local tech/contractor influencers and share their content (with value-add insights) to get on their radar.
3. **Local Contractor Network**: Actively connect with local interior designers, home builders, and AV integrators, offering to share *their* projects to your network.
4. **Community Value**: Join 3-5 Kansas City business/entrepreneur LinkedIn groups and answer questions related to home infrastructure/networking.

## 4-Week Content Plan (1-2 Posts/Week)
- **Week 1: Founder Identity & "Why KC"** (Positioning)
- **Week 2: Smart-Home Trend Insight** (Authority)
- **Week 3: The Infrastructure Gap** (Pain Points)
- **Week 4: Local Success/Community Case Study** (Trust)

## Scheduling & Automation
*Note: LinkedIn’s API has strict rules against fully automated posting from personal accounts. Options include:*
1. **Manual Posting**: Schedule your posts in a calendar (e.g., Google Calendar) and paste them into LinkedIn on the scheduled day. 
2. **Third-Party Schedulers**: Use tools like Buffer, Hootsuite, or FeedHive, which interface with LinkedIn’s official APIs to schedule personal posts.
3. **API Integration**: If you obtain a LinkedIn developer token, we can adapt the company `runner.py` script to handle personal posting, but manual review is highly recommended to ensure you can respond to comments immediately.
