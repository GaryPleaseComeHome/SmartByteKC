Our Story

Vince grew up in Overland Park, took apart every gadget in the house, and never quite stopped. A degree in control systems engineering led to designing automation for manufacturing — precision, redundancy, fail-safes. But the corporate world missed something: the human element.

The moment a family's Wi-Fi works for the first time in the basement office. The relief when a business owner realizes their data is actually backed up. That's the work that matters.

SmartByteKC launched in 2010 from a garage (classic, but true). Today it's a licensed, insured, full-service IT and low-voltage contractor serving the entire KC metro. Same mission: explain it clearly, engineer it right, show up when we say we will. No scripts. No tiers of support. Just Vince and a small team who treat your home or business like their own.
